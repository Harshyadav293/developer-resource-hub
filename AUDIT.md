# GDGoC AITR Portal Review & Suggestions

**Portal:** https://gdgocaitr.vercel.app/

## Review Summary

I reviewed the GDGoC AITR portal on desktop and mobile. **No major bugs or visible layout problems were found during my review.**

## What I Checked

- Links and navigation — No major issue found
- Text and alignment — Looks clear
- Mobile layout — Works properly
- Buttons and sections — Works properly
- Overall visual layout — Looks clean

## Suggestions for Improvement

1. Add a clearer section for upcoming events and important announcements.
2. Make event/resource discovery easier with simple categories or filters.
3. Keep navigation simple and mobile-friendly.
4. Improve accessibility with clear button labels, readable contrast, and image alt text.

## Conclusion

The portal is usable and I did not find any major bug. The suggestions above are improvements that could make the experience clearer and easier to use.
